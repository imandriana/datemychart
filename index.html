The premise

Okay, but... how does this work?

  1. 1

    You submit the charts.

    Send us your birth date, birth time, birthplace, and a little context about your relationship.

  2. 2

    We investigate.

    Ana and a guest judge dig into your synastry to uncover the chemistry, compatibility, conflict, and potential.

  3. 3

    We make the call.

    Will this relationship burn bright and burn out? Get messy? Or actually go the distance?

  4. 4

    We wait for the receipts.

    And if we can, we'll check back in to see whether the stars got it right.
